My name's Steve Rokitka (aka Rocketkar) and I'm a full-stack product designer. What's that mean exactly? I define it as someone with the diversity of skills and experience to look at a business need, research the problem space, then design, test and build solutions from prototype to production. In other words, an unapologetic generalist and eternal student.

My path's always been a multidisciplinary one. I started out as a journalist and illustrator, learned to code and do usability research at Cornell University, moved to Seattle and developed specializations within UI/UX, and enriched them with developer training as well as courses on color cognition, typography, business strategy, and brand development through IDEO and others.

In between UX jobs, I have a habit of embarking on projects that give me excuses to learn new skills. In recent years I overhauled and rode a motorcycle to Burning Man, got Wilderness First Responder certified, moved to an eccentric little island and adopted a rescue Malinois, and spun up my photography hobby into a business. Each of these things has given me a wider frame of reference, and also made me a more successful and empathetic designer.

Today, I'm looking for awesome projects that I can be a part of. If you've got something that needs solutions found, ideas given form, stories discovered or just a bit of snark injected into a situation, drop me a line and let's get started.
